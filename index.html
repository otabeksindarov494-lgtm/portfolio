Spendly

Flutter · Firebase · REST API

A mobile app that tracks work days, lateness and penalties for a monthly salary report, and keeps a record of debts and advances.

Flutter Developer

Flutter, Dart, Firebase, REST API

Overview

Spendly is a mobile app built with Flutter and Dart. It keeps a monthly report of the working period and a list of debts and advances, so the numbers that matter are in one place.

The app uses Firebase and connects to a REST API for its data.

What I built

Step-by-step onboarding

A multi-step setup (18 steps) collects the profile photo, which can be skipped, the expected salary range and the lunch break length. The salary and the break are used to calculate working hours and the hourly rate.

Monthly report and statistics

The report screen shows the calculated salary for the selected month, the base salary and the penalty, and a progress bar of worked days out of the working days. Cards below it show total worked hours, present days, absent days, total lateness, total early leave and total penalty.

Debts and advances

A summary shows what is owed to me and what I owe, with a net status. Entries are added by voice or manually, can be filtered as all, owed to me or I owe, and are marked when paid.

Navigation

A bottom bar connects the Home, Report, Chat, Debts and Profile screens.

paynet_app

Role

Let’s talk ↗GitHub ↗

Overview

paynet_app is a payment application inspired by Paynet, built with Flutter and Dart and focused on a modern, clean interface.

The interface follows a Paynet-inspired dark design with green accents.

What I built

Money requests

A request form to choose the recipient, enter the amount, pick the card that receives the money, add a comment or an image and send the request. A bottom sheet offers more actions, such as changing the amount or canceling the request.

Cards and confirmation

A card picker lists UzCard and Humo cards with their balances and lets the user add a card. Actions are confirmed with an SMS code and a resend timer.

Interface

A dark theme with green gradients and rounded cards, following a Paynet-inspired design.

Flutter · Bloc · Firebase

Overview

TrimApp helps clients book beauty and grooming services: haircuts, makeup, manicure and massage. Salons are shown in a list and on a map with their rating, distance and details.

The app uses Firebase and Cloud Firestore for its data, and the screens and their state are managed with flutter_bloc.

What I built

Registration and profile

Sign-up by phone number or email with a password and a terms checkbox. A profile page then saves the user’s details through a ProfileBloc, and once the profile is loaded the app opens the main navigation.

Home and search

A home screen with search, a promo banner, service categories, filter chips and a list of salons near the user with distance and rating.

Map

A Yandex Maps screen that finds the user’s location and shows a card for the selected salon with its rating, distance, a show-on-map button and a booking button.

Salon page and bookings

A salon page with photo, address, rating, quick actions (site, chat, call, location, share), the masters and a Book button. The My orders screen lists active, completed and canceled bookings, with a reminder switch, cancel and open receipt actions.

Responsive layout

Sizes are scaled with flutter_screenutil, so the screens keep the same proportions on different devices.
